## Layer 1 Source Note

Source claim: The 2024 J.P. Morgan update revisits concentrated-stock event risk and defines a catastrophic decline as a severe peak decline without recovery (Cembalest2024, p. 1).
Source claim: The report broadens the discussion beyond historical business failure by emphasizing recent macro shocks, COVID-era distortions, and rapid monetary tightening as channels through which individual firms can suffer large losses (Cembalest2024, pp. 1--2).
Source claim: The report treats interim catastrophic decline as relevant even when a stock may still have gains relative to IPO levels, because the intended perspective is that of a current concentrated holder (Cembalest2024, p. 1).
Commentary: This source supports the thesis motivation that catastrophic equity losses are investor-timing and portfolio-context events, not merely terminal bankruptcy events.
