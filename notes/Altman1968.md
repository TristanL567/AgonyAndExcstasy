## Layer 1 Source Note

Source claim: Altman frames bankruptcy prediction as a test case for whether financial ratio analysis can be made more rigorous with multivariate statistical classification rather than discarded as a practitioner heuristic (Altman1968, pp. 589--590).
Source claim: The paper develops a discriminant model on matched bankrupt and non-bankrupt manufacturing firms, so its empirical scope is deliberately narrower than a general listed-equity distress model (Altman1968, pp. 589--590, Section III).
Commentary: This source anchors the thesis literature review because it is the canonical starting point for accounting-ratio distress prediction, against which later hazard models and machine-learning approaches position themselves.
Caution: Its manufacturing-only sample and 1960s bankruptcy setting mean it should be treated as historical foundation, not as direct evidence for a modern CRSP-like index exclusion rule.
